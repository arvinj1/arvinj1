## What I care about

- **Real-time media:** predictable behavior across signaling, network, media, and application layers.
- **Distributed systems:** designing for failure, observability, scale, and operational ownership.
- **Engineering leadership:** creating teams and technical direction that turn client problems into durable platforms.
- **Practical AI tooling:** making agent workflows useful through clear boundaries, review, and measurable outcomes.

## How I think about systems

A fast component is not enough. The end-to-end experience depends on the behavior between components: timing, failure handling, interoperability, observability, and the people operating the system. I like working through those boundaries with engineers, product partners, and customers.
